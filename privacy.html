Debavox Privacy Policy

Effective: 31 October 2026 — operator details confirmed; technical compliance review pending

Debavox is a UK-based platform for finding and reading public social-media conversations, including Bluesky and Mastodon, with YouTube integration planned. This notice distinguishes existing processing from planned YouTube functionality. This text must be checked against the deployed implementation before representing it as final.

Controller: Mark Loughran, operating Debavox as an individual in the United Kingdom.

Privacy contact: loughranmark33@gmail.com (public contact method: email).

Postal address: Not published on this website; any additional contact particulars required by applicable law remain subject to review.

What information we process

We may handle registration identifiers, email addresses, account preferences, user-submitted questions, saved/followed conversations and support enquiries through existing account, login and save/follow features; technical usage, security and login information (including IP addresses); and public third-party discussion material, such as handles, comments, dates, links and reply counts.

Public comments and opinions may constitute personal data. Discussion of religion, politics or other sensitive topics may also reveal special-category information; public availability alone does not eliminate data protection obligations.

Purposes and legal bases

We use relevant information to provide account services and user-requested features (contract, where applicable), discover and organise public conversations and protect the service (legitimate interests, subject to balancing and additional special-category conditions where necessary), respond to legal obligations (legal obligation), and operate optional tracking or communications only with appropriate consent where required. Specific assessments and any relevant special-category condition must be documented before relying on them.

YouTube API Services

Planned integration: Debavox is applying to use YouTube API Services to discover public videos and their public comment discussions, display limited attributed previews, and link users back to the original material on YouTube. This integration is not yet launched; this description explains the intended processing when it becomes available. The planned public-data integration will not require visitors to connect or sign in to a Google or YouTube account. Debavox is not sponsored or endorsed by Google or YouTube.

YouTube information and purposes: For public discovery, we expect to retrieve and temporarily process video identifiers, titles, descriptions, channel names and identifiers, links, public comments and commenter display names where available, publication times and publicly exposed engagement information. We would use that information to select relevant public discussions and show source-attributed links and previews. Public YouTube material may still contain personal information. We do not plan to access private YouTube accounts, watch history, private subscriptions or Google login credentials through this integration, or to post on anyone's behalf.

Storage and refresh: Our intended implementation will delete or refresh temporarily stored non-authorised YouTube API Data within 30 calendar days, and account for content that has been changed or removed on YouTube. We will not download or host YouTube videos or audio. A technical verification of these controls, including existing general conversation storage, is required before this integration goes live.

Requests for removal: Contact loughranmark33@gmail.com to request access to, correction of or deletion of personal information held by Debavox, including a YouTube-attributed preview if applicable. We will assess applicable requests and remove qualifying YouTube-related personal information held by Debavox promptly, within seven calendar days where required by YouTube's policies. Removing Debavox's stored information does not delete the original material from YouTube; requests to remove the original content must be made through YouTube. We will provide a functional request-and-deletion process before enabling YouTube integration.

Google account permissions: The planned integration does not use Google user sign-in or OAuth authorisation. If account-authorised YouTube features are ever introduced, we will update this policy, seek appropriate consent and provide an access-revocation and deletion process. Authorised access can also be reviewed through the Google permissions page.

YouTube Terms: https://www.youtube.com/t/terms

Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Google permissions (where account authorisation is ever enabled): https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions

YouTube API information is subject to Google's platform-specific display, refresh and deletion rules. This notice does not supersede those requirements. The site's actual data-retention mechanisms must be checked and updated to meet them.
